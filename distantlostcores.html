Distant Lost Cores – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Android app com.distantlostcores

Distant Lost Cores ("the game") is an idle space game developed and published by Rostislav M ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game handles, who receives it, and what choices you have.

Google (AdMob and Google Play) is the only third party that receives data from your device.

1. Data on your device

Your progress (planets, upgrades, weapons, gems, relics, achievements), the purchases you have made, and your settings (language, music volume, frame rate, effects) are saved in the game's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it and it is never transmitted. Clearing the game's data or uninstalling the game deletes it. The game does not use Android cloud backup.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC, USA) using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. An ad plays only when you tap a button to watch one for an in-game reward. The game is fully playable without ads, and a one-time purchase removes them.

When an ad is requested, shown or tapped, the SDK may send Google:

  • your resettable Advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or region) can be inferred;
  • device and app information: model, Android version, language, screen size, network type, app package name and version;
  • ad interactions: ads requested, shown, completed or tapped.

Google uses this to select and deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud, and, only if you consent, personalise them. Google is an independent controller of this data. We never receive it; we see only aggregated, anonymous ad reports. See the Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and the list of ad technology providers.

3. Ad consent and your controls

Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states with privacy laws), Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) appears before ads load. You can decline personalised ads; ads are then non-personalised or not shown, and the game still works fully. Google's SDK stores your choice on your device. Where the law gives you the right to change your mind, Settings → Ad privacy options reopens the form.

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Opt out of personalised ads: the same screen, or adssettings.google.com.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers optional purchases (gem packs, removing ads, weapon bundles). Google Play processes them; we never see or store card or payment details. Google Play tells the game which product was bought (product ID and order ID). The game uses this only to unlock the item and to restore it after a reinstall or when you tap "Restore purchases". Your Google account, payment method and purchase history are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds go through Google Play.

The game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, notifications or any other sensitive permission.

6. Third-party software

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform SDK: ads and consent (sections 2 and 3).
  • Google Play Billing Library: purchases (section 4).
  • Godot Engine: the open-source engine the game is built with; it collects nothing.

Google Play also gives us aggregated, anonymous statistics (installs, crashes, ratings, sales) under Google's own policy. They cannot identify you.

7. Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, we are the controller of the data in sections 1 and 4 that stays on your device, and Google is an independent controller of the advertising and payment data in sections 2 and 4. Our legal bases:

  • Personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention, ad measurement: legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)), unless you object in the consent form.
  • Local game data and purchases: needed to provide the game and the items you bought (Art. 6(1)(b)).

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing, and complain to your supervisory authority. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete; use My Google Account and the controls in section 3 for Google's data. You can still e-mail us and we will point you to the right place. Google may process advertising data outside the EEA/UK, including in the United States, under the safeguards on its privacy frameworks page (EU–US Data Privacy Framework, Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. US state privacy rights

In California and other US states with privacy laws, sharing your Advertising ID with Google to show personalised ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out in the consent form shown in those states, in Settings → Ad privacy options where offered, or by deleting your Advertising ID in Android settings. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will help.

10. Retention and security

Data on your device stays there until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. Google keeps advertising and payment data under its own retention policies; resetting your Advertising ID limits the advertising data. We retain nothing. Android isolates the game's storage from other apps, and the ad SDK and Google Play Billing talk to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

11. Changes

If the game later handles data differently (for example analytics, cloud save or online features), we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships, and note material changes in the Google Play release notes. The current version is always at the address linked from the game's Google Play listing.
