Distant Lost Cores – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.distantlostcores

Distant Lost Cores ("the game") is an idle space game developed and published by Rostislav M ("we", "us", "the developer"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, who receives it, and what choices you have.

Summary

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (planets, upgrades, weapons, gems, relics, achievements), purchases you have made, and settings (language, music volume, frame rate, effects, ad-consent choice) are saved in a file inside the game's private, sandboxed storage. No other app can read it, and it is never transmitted anywhere. Clearing the game's data in Android settings, or uninstalling the game, deletes it permanently. The game does not use Android cloud backup.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA), using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. An ad only plays when you tap a button to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. The game is fully playable without ever watching an ad, and a one-time purchase removes ads.

What the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect

When an ad is requested, shown, or interacted with, the SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • your device's Advertising ID (the resettable Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or region) can be inferred;
  • device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, app package name and version;
  • ad interaction data: which ads were requested, shown, watched to completion, or tapped.

Google uses this information to select and deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, only if you have consented, to personalise the ads you see. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. We never receive, see, or store it; we only see aggregated, anonymous reporting (for example, the number of ads shown). Google describes its handling of this data in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The list of advertising partners Google may work with when serving ads is published at Ad technology providers.

Consent

Before any ad is loaded, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) where the law requires it: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland under the GDPR/UK GDPR, and in US states with consumer-privacy laws. You can decline personalised advertising there. If you decline, ads are either non-personalised or not shown at all, and the game continues to work fully.

Where the law grants you the right to change your mind, the game's Settings screen shows an Ad privacy options button that reopens the same form at any time.

Your controls outside the game

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Google → Ads). Deleting the ID stops any app from receiving it.
  • Opt out of ad personalisation: the same Android screen, or adssettings.google.com.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers optional in-app purchases (for example gem packs, removing ads, and weapon bundles). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play using the Google Play Billing Library. We never see or store your card or payment details, and no payment data passes through the game. After a purchase, Google Play tells the game which product was bought (product ID and order ID). The game uses this only to unlock the item and to restore it if you reinstall or tap "Restore purchases". Your Google account, payment method and purchase history are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

4. Notifications

The game does not send notifications of any kind, and it does not request the notification permission.

6. Third-party software in the game

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): ad delivery, section 2.
  • Google User Messaging Platform SDK: the consent form, section 2. It stores your consent choice on your device.
  • Google Play Billing Library: in-app purchases, section 3.
  • Godot Engine: the open-source game engine the game is built with. It collects nothing.

Separately from the game, Google Play provides us with aggregated, anonymous statistics about installs, uninstalls, crashes, ratings and sales under Google's own privacy policy. We cannot identify individual users from them.

7. Legal basis and your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

For the purposes of the GDPR and UK GDPR, the developer is the controller for the game's own processing described in sections 1 and 3 (which never leaves your device), and Google LLC is an independent controller for the advertising and payment data described in sections 2 and 3.

  • Personalised advertising: processed on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via the Ad privacy options button in Settings.
  • Non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention and ad measurement: processed on the basis of Google's and our legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)), unless you have objected via the consent form.
  • Local game data and purchases: necessary to provide the game you have chosen to install and the items you bought (Art. 6(1)(b)).

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, or receive a copy of personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to hand over or delete; for advertising and payment data, exercise these rights directly with Google via My Google Account or the controls in section 2. You can still e-mail us and we will help you find the right place.

International transfers. Google processes advertising data on servers that may be outside the EEA/UK, including in the United States, under the safeguards described on the Google privacy frameworks page (EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. US state privacy rights

If you live in California or another US state with a consumer-privacy law, the sharing of your Advertising ID with Google for the purpose of showing personalised ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under that law. You can opt out through the consent form the game shows in those states, through the Ad privacy options button in Settings where offered, or by deleting your Advertising ID in Android settings. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

9. Children

Distant Lost Cores is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Data retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the game's data or uninstall the game. Advertising and payment data is retained by Google according to its own retention policies; you can shorten advertising data retention by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID. We retain nothing.

11. Security

Game data lives in the game's private, sandboxed storage that Android isolates from other apps. All communication between the Google Mobile Ads SDK or Google Play Billing and Google's servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

12. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game handles data differently (for example an analytics SDK, cloud save, or online features), we will update this page, change the effective date at the top, and, for material changes, note it in the game's release notes on Google Play. The current version is always at the address linked from the game's Google Play listing.
